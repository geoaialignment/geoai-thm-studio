# Step: physics
H groundwater flow; T heat conduction; TH heat with flow; HC solute transport (one component); HM consolidation;
TM thermal stress; THM heat, flow and deformation. Gravity is on for flow in vertical sections, and off for mechanics
(v0.1 has no initial stress state). For HC give the component: pore_diffusion, half_life (decay) and retardation.
For salt water that sinks under its own weight (density-driven flow), give the water property density_increase_per_concentration (kg/m3 per unit concentration, e.g. 200 for 1200 kg/m3 at c = 1).
