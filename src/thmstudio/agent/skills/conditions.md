# Step: conditions
Boundaries without a condition are closed (no flow, no heat flux). Give at least one fixed head/pressure and, for
steady heat, one fixed temperature. Water inflow: flux (m/s per area, inflow positive) or rate (m3/s total; negative =
pumping). Heat: flux (W/m2) or rate (W). Heat sources: power_density (W/m3) or power (W, the whole source).
Mechanics: roller on boundaries that must not move across (usually bottom and sides), load = normal pressure
(compression positive). Every mechanical model must be held in both directions.
Initial values: temperature, and head or pressure.
