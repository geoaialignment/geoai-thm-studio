# Water (the default fluid)

Defaults: density 1000 kg/m3, viscosity 1.0e-3 Pa s (about 20 °C), specific heat 4180 J/kg/K, thermal conductivity 0.6 W/m/K,
volumetric thermal expansion 4.0e-4 1/K (water near 50 °C; about 2.1e-4 1/K at 20 °C).
Properties are constant in v0.1; temperature-dependent viscosity and density are not modelled.
For heating problems the expansion value matters: thermal pressurisation scales with it.
